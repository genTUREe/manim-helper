Questions

What is Manim?

Manim is a Python animation library for making precise visuals. Fieldnote gives you a small place to try an idea and see what a few lines of animation can do.

Who can join?

Anyone 18 or under, anywhere in the world. It is free to take part.

Can I join without knowing Python?

Absolutely. Start with an example, change one thing, and learn as you go. You do not need a finished plan before you begin.

What counts as math?

Anything you can explain with a picture: a pattern, a proof, probability, physics, or a surprising connection you noticed.

What do I get?

After you share your video and a person reviews it, we mail you something real. The object list will be published on opening day.

Where do I get help?

Drop into Fieldnote Commons, our friendly help room for questions, sketches, and debugging together.
